# Task: write the desk handoff

Desk `{{SECTION_KEY}}` has been judged complete. Write the bounded handoff that
the research principal will read. It is the only thing that leaves this desk, so
it must stand alone.

Read:

{{CONTEXT_FILES}}

Stay under 500 words and 8192 bytes. Do not summarise the transcript; state what
another desk or the principal needs in order to act.

## Respond with exactly these Markdown headings and nothing else

## Outcome
## Decisions
## Interfaces
## Risks
## What is unproven
## Next action

Under **Outcome**, what this desk established: how many records, to what
standard of evidence, and the two or three findings that would change someone's
mind. Not activity. "Swept four registers" is activity; "eleven live
proceedings, six with primary-sourced case numbers, two with an asset explicitly
advertised" is an outcome.

Under **Decisions**, the judgement calls another desk must inherit: which source
you treated as primary for this jurisdiction, how you resolved an entity that
appears under several names, where you drew the line on what counts as a
software asset.

Under **Interfaces**, anything another desk now depends on: schema fields you
added, stage vocabulary you extended, entity identifiers other desks must match
on, comparables you contributed.

Under **Risks**, what could still go wrong and what would reveal it. Staleness
belongs here: name the records whose stage decays fastest and when they should
be re-verified.

`What is unproven` is the heading that costs you something, so write it first
and cut it last. List every claim this desk makes that has not been settled
against a primary source: a stage taken from an aggregator, a revenue figure
from a secondary report, an administrator named by one source only, an asset
described in a listing nobody has confirmed still exists, a price nobody
published. Name the observation that would settle each one. `- None; every
claim above rests on a primary source quoted in the record` is a legitimate
answer and you must be able to defend it.

A reader above you treats this handoff as a claim and goes looking for the
record that proves it. Anything you leave out here, they will find.
